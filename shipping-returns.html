Islamabad Delivery & Returns Policy

Transparent surplus shopping requires knowing both how your parcel will arrive in Islamabad and what happens if an item does not match its documented condition grade.

Important Pre-Launch Notice: While GoodAgain’s business plan establishes Islamabad as the initial launch market and commits to documented returns, specific delivery charges, sector cut-off times, and return day counts below are editable draft values recorded in CONTENT-TODO.md for owner confirmation before launch.

1. Delivery Coverage & Sample Rates

  • Initial Launch Geography: Controlled local delivery across Islamabad Capital Territory (sectors, university hostels, and residential areas to be finalized in WooCommerce shipping zones).
  • Prototype Sample Delivery Calculation: The demo cart uses a sample flat rate of Rs. 250 for standard Islamabad delivery and Rs. 0 (Free Demo Delivery) on sample orders of Rs. 5,000 or above.
  • Coordinated Campus / Hub Drop-Off (Draft Concept): The prototype also demonstrates a Rs. 0 campus/hub drop-off option for university student batches, subject to operational approval.
  • Dispatch Target: The business plan sets an operational SMART target of dispatching at least 90% of verified local orders on time.

2. Inspecting Your Parcel Upon Arrival

Every GoodAgain order record stores the specific SKU, Condition Grade (A, B, or C), and disclosed accessory list selected at checkout. When your parcel arrives:

  1. Check that the outer shipping seal is intact before opening.
  2. Compare the delivered item and included accessories against the Condition Panel summary on your order confirmation.
  3. Test the item within the approved post-delivery inspection window and keep the original packaging and SKU tag until you are satisfied.

3. Return Eligibility Matrix (Draft Policy for Approval)

Because surplus and open-box items may have pre-disclosed cosmetic marks or missing non-essential accessories, return eligibility distinguishes between undisclosed discrepancies and already-disclosed grade characteristics:
